# FutureOS — Decision Field

FutureOS should feel like a living research instrument: rigorous enough to trust,
editorial enough to understand, and calm enough to use for consequential work.

## Reference set

These references informed principles, not surface imitation.

1. [AI in Design Report 2026](https://stateofaidesign.com/) — large editorial
   hierarchy, disciplined grids, mono annotations, and data treated as a story.
2. [Linear](https://linear.app/) via Godly and SiteInspire — the product surface is
   the hero; restraint and progressive focus create authority.
3. [Mintlify](https://www.mintlify.com/) via Recent — a complex infrastructure
   product can combine a strong metaphor, serif contrast, and legible UI proof.
4. [Aave Cards](https://recent.design/i/0pqbmvl-aave-cards) via Recent — metrics
   become memorable when each visualization communicates one fact.
5. [Sunrise Robotics](https://www.sunriserobotics.co/) via SiteInspire — technical
   confidence can come from geometry, scale, and whitespace instead of effects.

Microsoft AI, OpenCall AI, CSS Design Awards, Awwwards, The FWA, and the broader
Godly and SiteInspire collections were used as counterchecks for category
conventions and overused AI patterns.

## Original idea: Decision Field

A decision is represented as a probability field that changes over time. Evidence
creates a visible node; the forecast moves; the previous path remains faintly
visible. The interface never pretends uncertainty disappears—it makes movement
and resolution legible.

The field is expressed through:

- measurement grids and reference lines;
- probability trajectories rather than decorative waves;
- mono labels for provenance, timing, and system state;
- editorial headlines for human questions;
- high-contrast instrument panels for active decisions;
- signal green only for actionable evidence and live state;
- coral only for reversal triggers, misses, and exceptions.

## Core tokens

- Bone `#F2EFE6`: the reading surface.
- Ink `#0B1511`: authority, focus, and product instrumentation.
- Signal `#D4FF4F`: evidence, action thresholds, and live probability.
- Signal dark `#3D6800`: accessible annotation text on light surfaces.
- Coral `#F06A4D`: exceptions and negative outcomes.

Typography has three jobs:

- sans serif: product action and navigation;
- editorial serif italic: human judgment and uncertainty;
- monospace: measurements, provenance, formulas, and system state.

## Interaction rules

1. Motion must explain a state change: probability trace, evidence arrival, or
   resolution. Decorative looping is excluded.
2. Every active decision exposes the question, forecast, threshold, deadline,
   and reversal condition before secondary metadata.
3. Each chart communicates one proposition and remains understandable without
   animation.
4. Sharp outer geometry signals the system; softer internal controls signal the
   human action inside it.
5. Reduced-motion preferences remove tracing and pulsing without hiding data.

## Deliberate exclusions

- no generic purple AI gradients;
- no glassmorphism as a substitute for hierarchy;
- no floating 3D object without product meaning;
- no mascot-led tone for consequential decisions;
- no dashboard wall of equally weighted cards;
- no mathematical notation used as decoration.
