# FutureOS Six-Week Evidence Cadence

**Baseline locked:** 2026-08-31

**Final checkpoint:** 2026-10-12

This cadence exists to prove continuity, not to manufacture favorable results. Every checkpoint must be published even when the result is zero, negative, or inconclusive.

| Checkpoint | Date | Required artifact | Minimum auditable content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 2026-08-31 | Benchmark v0.1 + preregistration draft | Version, claim boundary, current zero-state, open risks |
| Week 1 | 2026-09-07 | Clarity note | Observed friction, denominator, shipped fixes or explicit no-change decision |
| Week 2 | 2026-09-14 | Activation snapshot | Invited → activated funnel; ineligible/excluded counts; no inferred impact |
| Week 3 | 2026-09-21 | First update audit | Evidence-linked updates, reminders used, attrition, first deviation log |
| Week 4 | 2026-09-28 | Teaching artifact | Workshop kit version, delivery status, pre/post instrument, privacy rule |
| Week 5 | 2026-10-05 | Resolution audit | Due vs. not-due records, resolved denominator, Brier eligibility |
| Week 6 | 2026-10-12 | Public learning report | Full funnel, feedback-led releases, null findings, limitations, next hypothesis |

## Weekly immutable bundle

Each checkpoint preserves:

1. a dated aggregate export or explicit zero-state;
2. release or commit reference;
3. one assumption tested;
4. one observation;
5. one decision made from the observation;
6. one limitation or failed expectation;
7. a claim-audit result.

## Admission-safe language

Use:

> Built and maintained a six-week open evidence program; published methods, weekly zero-to-resolution funnels, negative findings, and product changes traceable to observed behavior.

Do not use numerical placeholders until the evidence ledger supports them.
