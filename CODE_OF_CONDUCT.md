# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim to maintain a thoughtful, evidence-seeking, and welcoming project. Be respectful, critique ideas rather than people, state uncertainty honestly, and protect the privacy of users and contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, manipulation of project metrics, and using contribution channels for spam.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
